ClearMovie 0.0.4 lets you make upright and square videos, record your own voice over a video, and set up any frame video, not only a green one. Videos can now have their own name, the preview plays smoothly, and the timeline is easier to work with.

## What's new

### Home and presets

- **No preset to begin with.** A new install of ClearMovie has no preset. Tap **New preset** to make your own, or **Start without a preset**. If you already use ClearMovie, your Event frame preset stays, and it is now a preset like any other: you can change it, or delete it if you no longer want it. Because it is your own preset now, **Reset preset** is gone.
- **A tidier home screen.** Your presets come first, then **Start without a preset**, then **New preset** (with no presets yet, **New preset** comes first). The cards are all the same size.
- **Choose the shape of a new preset.** When you make a preset, choose whether its videos are **Landscape** (wide, like a TV), **Portrait** (tall, like a phone held upright) or **Square**. Choose a frame and an intro made for that shape; ClearMovie tells you if a file is another shape.
- **Keep a copy of the files, or not.** A new preset now uses your intro, frame and ending files where they are on the phone, so they take no extra space. Leave them where they are: if you move or delete one, ClearMovie asks you for it again. Turn on **Keep a copy of the files** if you would rather ClearMovie kept its own copies.
- **Set up the frame.** When you choose a preset's frame file, ClearMovie asks you to tap the screen colour to remove (green, or another strong colour), and to drag a box to where your photos and videos should show. **Colour and area** in the preset changes it later.
- **Pick how captions come in.** A preset's caption style has a new **Animation** tab: new captions can fade, zoom, pop, bounce or slide in, or simply appear.

### In the editor

- **Choose the shape of your video.** A video started without a preset asks **What shape should your video be?** the first time it opens, with the shape of your first photo or video already chosen. You can change it later from **More options** (the three dots at the top), then **Video shape**. Videos you made before stay wide.
- **Give your video a name.** Tap the title at the top, or choose **More options**, then **Rename video**. The name is shown in My videos. Undo takes it back.
- **A simpler top bar.** At the top are Back, the title, Undo and Redo side by side, and **More options** with **Rename video**, **Video shape** and **Settings**.
- **Export is on the row with Play.** The **Export** button, with an upward arrow, is now at the right end of the row under the preview, on the same row as **Play**. It opens **Save your video** as before.
- **A smooth preview.** The preview now plays smoothly, with the frame, the transitions and the captions in place. The **As exported** button is gone, because the preview now shows the video as it will be saved.
- **Record your voice.** On a voice row, tap **+**, then **Record voice**. After a short countdown the video plays without its own sound while you speak, and your words are lined up with the picture. Tap **Stop** when you are done. The first time, ClearMovie asks to use the microphone. Your recording stays on the phone, inside ClearMovie.
- **Set up a frame on the timeline.** Tap a frame, then **Colour** to choose the colour to remove, or **Area** to place your photos and videos in it. A frame you add opens this by itself.
- **A different frame for part of the video.** The frame row now has a **+** too: it starts a new frame where the line on the timeline is, and the frame before it stops there.

### On the timeline

- **Scroll the rows.** Swipe up and down to see every row, and sideways to move through the video. With the phone held sideways, at least four rows now show at once, with slimmer bars.
- **A + at the end of every row.** Each row has a **+** at its right end, in the same place on every row. It adds at the line on the timeline or, when something is already there, right after it. When you select an item, its own **+** on each side shows instead.
- **Handles on every item.** A selected caption, frame, music, voice or small picture now has a handle at each end, like photos and videos. Drag one to make the item start or end earlier or later. For a caption, **Match item** makes it fit its photo or video again.
- **Lower for talk, your way.** **Lower for talk** opens a small window: switch it on and choose how quiet the music gets while people talk, from 0 to 100%.

### Captions

- **New words to start with.** A new caption now starts with the words "The image caption". Type your own words in their place.
- **Change the size of the caption box.** Drag the handles on a caption's box to make it wider or narrower (and taller, when there is room). The words wrap to fit, at the same size.

### Settings

- **Presets are on the home screen only.** Settings no longer lists presets. Change one with the pencil next to it on the home screen.
- **The version opens the ClearMovie page.** Tap **Open ClearMovie on GitHub** under the version to see the project in your browser.

## Which file to download

There is one file, `ClearMovie-0.0.4.apk`, and it works on every kind of phone. Open it on your phone to install it. If you have an older version, it installs as an update, so there is no need to remove the old version first, and your videos and presets stay. ClearMovie needs Android 10 or newer.

If you use [Obtainium](https://obtainium.imranr.dev/) and your ClearMovie entry still has the APK filter `arm64-v8a`, edit the entry and clear that filter. Otherwise Obtainium will not find the new file.

## Good to know

- The intro, frame and ending videos are still not part of the app. Copy your own onto the phone first, then choose them when ClearMovie asks.
- A preset you delete, the Event frame included, is gone for good. Videos you already made with it keep working.
- Only one video can be saved at a time.
- If something looks wrong, please tell us on the [ClearMovie GitHub page](https://github.com/itsluminous/ClearMovie/issues).
