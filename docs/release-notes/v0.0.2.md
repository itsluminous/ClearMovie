ClearMovie 0.0.2 lets you make your own presets, and gives each preset its own look for captions. It also comes as one file that works on every phone.

## What's new

- **Make your own presets.** On the home screen, tap **New preset**. Give it a name, choose whether it has an intro and a frame, and pick its ending: a spoken message, a video, or no ending. Then choose its files from your phone. Those parts stay the same once the preset is made, because the videos you make from it use them.
- **Change a preset.** Tap the pencil next to a preset on the home screen, or open it from **Settings**. You can rename it, choose different files, and change its captions. Changes apply to the videos you make after that. Videos you already made keep their own look.
- **Delete a preset you made.** It is no longer offered for new videos, and the videos you already made with it keep working.
- **The Event frame preset can be changed, and put back.** It can't be deleted. If you change it, **Reset preset** brings back its name, words and caption style as ClearMovie ships them. The files you chose for it stay.
- **Each preset has its own caption look.** Choose the words a new caption starts with, where it sits, its size, font and colour, and whether it has an outline or a band behind it. Once the preset's frame file is chosen, a picture of the frame shows how the caption will look while you change it.
- **A new look for Event frame captions.** A new caption now shows as plain white words on the blue strip near the bottom of the frame, with no outline or dark band behind them, and starts with the words "सुंदर प्रकृति". You can change the words, or the whole look, for one caption or for the preset.

## Which file to download

There is now just one file, `ClearMovie-0.0.2.apk`, and it works on every phone. Open it on your phone to install it. If you have 0.0.1, it installs as an update, so there is no need to remove 0.0.1 first. ClearMovie needs Android 10 or newer.

If you use [Obtainium](https://obtainium.imranr.dev/) and your ClearMovie entry has the APK filter `arm64-v8a`, edit the entry and clear that filter. Otherwise Obtainium will not find the new file.

## Good to know

- The intro, frame and ending videos are still not part of the app. Copy your own onto the phone first, then choose them when ClearMovie asks.
- Videos can't be given their own name yet. In My videos they are listed by date.
- Only one video can be saved at a time.
- If something looks wrong, please tell us on the [ClearMovie GitHub page](https://github.com/itsluminous/ClearMovie/issues).
