This is the first version of ClearMovie, a simple video maker for Android phones. It turns the photos and videos from a wedding, a birthday or any family event into one finished video, with an intro at the start, a frame around your pictures and a spoken message at the end. Everything happens on your phone. ClearMovie never uses the internet, and it adds no watermark.

## What you can do

- **Start from a preset.** The Event frame preset plays an intro video first, puts a frame around your photos and videos (they show through its green window, and the frame's own music plays quietly underneath), and ends with a spoken message over your last photo or video. You choose its three files on your phone once. For any one video you can replace or skip the intro, the frame or the ending. You can also start without a preset.
- **Share from your gallery.** Select photos and videos in your gallery, tap Share and choose ClearMovie. They go onto the timeline in the order they were taken, as far as the phone knows. You can also add them from inside the app.
- **Arrange everything on the timeline.** Drag items to change the order, or use Move left and Move right. Tap an item for big buttons with names under them, such as Trim, Split, Speed, Loop, Duration, Volume, Fit, Duplicate and Delete, and Rotate for photos. Pinch, or use the zoom buttons, to see more or less. The "+" before and after each item adds a photo, a video, or a card with words on a plain colour. Undo and Redo are always at the top.
- **Add more rows.** Words, small pictures in a corner, another frame, background music or someone speaking can each go on a row of their own. Background music is turned down automatically while your videos play with their own sound.
- **Transitions and animations.** Go from one item to the next with a crossfade, a fade through black or white, a wipe, a slide, a circle or a zoom. Let photos zoom in gently, and let words fade, slide or pop in. Each choice plays a short preview.
- **Captions.** Type your words, then drag them into place and change the size, font and colour, with an outline or a band behind them. Hindi and English fonts are built in, so Hindi letters look right.
- **Save and share.** Choose Best (for a TV), Good (for phones) or Small (the quickest to send on WhatsApp). The video is saved to your gallery, in the ClearMovie album, and keeps saving if you turn the phone or leave the app. Then tap Play or Share.
- **Your work is kept.** Changes are saved as you go, and if the app closes unexpectedly your video comes back.
- **Your language, your look.** You can choose English, Hindi or Hinglish inside the app. ClearMovie is light or dark to match your phone, and on Android 12 and newer it takes its colours from your wallpaper. Turn the phone sideways and the preview sits beside the timeline.

## Which file to download

Most phones need the file whose name ends in `arm64-v8a.apk`. If you are not sure, the one ending in `universal.apk` works on every phone but is a bigger download. ClearMovie needs Android 10 or newer.

## Good to know

- The intro, frame and ending videos are not part of the app. Copy your own onto the phone first (the Download folder is fine), then choose them when ClearMovie asks.
- There is one preset for now, Event frame.
- Videos can't be given their own name yet. In My videos they are listed by date.
- Only one video can be saved at a time.
- This is a first version, so a few things may still be rough. If something looks wrong, please tell us on the [ClearMovie GitHub page](https://github.com/itsluminous/ClearMovie/issues).
- ClearMovie is not on the Play Store or F-Droid yet, and it does not update itself. The [Obtainium](https://obtainium.imranr.dev/) app can check for new versions for you.
