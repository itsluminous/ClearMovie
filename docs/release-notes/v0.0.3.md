ClearMovie 0.0.3 fixes a problem in saved videos. Nothing else in the app has changed.

## What's fixed

- **The frame no longer disappears for a moment.** In a saved video, the frame around your photos and videos could disappear for a moment just after something started or ended: a photo or video, a transition, or a caption. You may never have noticed it; we saw it while testing ClearMovie. In our tests, videos saved with 0.0.3 keep the frame from start to end.

## Which file to download

There is one file, `ClearMovie-0.0.3.apk`, and it works on every kind of phone. Open it on your phone to install it. If you have 0.0.1 or 0.0.2, it installs as an update, so there is no need to remove the old version first. ClearMovie needs Android 10 or newer.

If you use [Obtainium](https://obtainium.imranr.dev/) and your ClearMovie entry still has the APK filter `arm64-v8a`, edit the entry and clear that filter. Otherwise Obtainium will not find the new file.

## Good to know

- Videos you saved with an older version may still have the problem. To save one again without the problem, open it from **My videos**, tap **Export**, then tap **Save video**.
- The intro, frame and ending videos are still not part of the app. Copy your own onto the phone first, then choose them when ClearMovie asks.
- Videos can't be given their own name yet. In My videos they are listed by date.
- Only one video can be saved at a time.
- If something looks wrong, please tell us on the [ClearMovie GitHub page](https://github.com/itsluminous/ClearMovie/issues).
