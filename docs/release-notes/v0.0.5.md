ClearMovie 0.0.5 can say your voice recordings in another voice, lets a preset choose the animation, transition and voice new videos start with, and lets you delete a video you no longer want. A frame you add now plays its own sound, sounds go quieter in layers under each other, and you can tap a photo, video or caption on the timeline to go to that moment.

## What's new

### Presets

- **Choose how new photos move, and the transition.** Edit preset has a new **Animation and transition** card. **Default animation** is how each new photo moves while it shows, and **Default transition** is how each new item moves into the next. You can still change each one in the editor.
- **Choose a default voice.** Edit preset has a **Default voice** card. New voice recordings in a video made from that preset start in this voice. **No change** keeps your own voice, and you can still change the voice of each recording in the editor.
- **Checking, not copying.** When a preset uses your files where they are, setup now says **Checking the file** instead of **Copying the file**. **Copying the file** shows only when ClearMovie really keeps its own copy. When a preset that uses your files where they are still has to copy one, ClearMovie tells you why: the place you chose the file from does not let ClearMovie open it again later, or ClearMovie already uses as many files where they are as Android allows.

### In the editor

- **Say it in another voice.** On a voice row, tap **+**. First choose a **Voice in the video**: a British, Indian or American voice, male or female. **Spoken in** then shows below it; for Hindi, set it to Hindi before you record (only the Indian voices say Hindi). Then tap **Record voice** and speak, or tap **Type the words** and write them. Spoken words are found on the phone and shown to you to check, then tap **Make voice**. Your own recording is always kept.
- **Change the voice later.** Tap a voice item, then **Change voice**. To hear your own voice again, choose **No change** and tap **Use my recording**.
- **The voices need the free voice pack, once.** When you first choose a voice, ClearMovie asks for the voices (about 110 MB to download). After your first recording it asks once more, for the words file of its language (about 40 MB); typed words do not need it. Each time, tap **Download**, download the files the page lists, come back and tap **Add the downloaded files**. ClearMovie shows the exact sizes, and the pack takes more room once added. After that the voices work without the internet, and nothing you say or type leaves the phone.
- **Delete a video.** **More options** (the three dots at the top), then **Delete video**. ClearMovie asks first. The video goes from My videos for good, but a video you already saved to your gallery stays there, and so do your own photos and videos. While a video is being saved to the gallery, wait for it to finish, or stop it, before you delete.
- **A frame you add plays its own sound.** A frame you add on a frame row now plays the sound of its video, in the preview and in the saved video. If you used such a frame in a video before, it plays its sound now too. The frame that comes with your preset is unchanged: its music still plays on the Music row, as before.
- **Volume and Lower for talk for a frame's sound.** Tap a frame you added whose video has sound to see **Volume** and **Lower for talk**. Touch and hold the frame row's icon for **Mute row** and **Lower frame sound under other sound**. A frame whose video has no sound has none of these.
- **Sounds go quieter in layers.** A frame's own sound goes quieter under your music, your music goes quieter under the sound of your videos, and all of them go quieter under a voice you recorded. The closing message still lowers everything. A video with no sound of its own no longer makes the music dip. To keep your videos' own sound up under a voice, tap a video, then **Lower for talk**, and switch it off; this keeps the sound of all the videos on that row up, not only that one. Touching and holding the photos and videos row's icon, then **Lower video sound under voices**, does the same. Music or a voice turned all the way down, or on a muted row, lowers nothing.

### On the timeline

- **Tap an item to go there.** While the video plays, tap any item on the timeline, and the video carries on from the place you tapped. While it is stopped, tap a photo, video, caption, frame or small picture, and the preview shows that moment. Tapping still selects the item, as before. While it is stopped, tapping music, a voice or another sound only selects it, and so does tapping an item on a row that is switched off.
- **The timeline follows along.** While the video plays, the timeline keeps moving with it after a tap. It stops following when you swipe it sideways, pinch it, or move or hold an item. Tap an item or the times above the rows, or tap Play again, and it follows again.
- **Less empty space at the start.** The video now starts right after the row icons, with only a small gap before 0:00.

### Settings

- **The voice pack in Storage.** Settings shows how much room the voice pack takes, with **Remove** to free it. Voices you already made stay in your videos.

## Which file to download

There is one file, `ClearMovie-0.0.5.apk`, and it works on every kind of phone. Open it on your phone to install it. If you have an older version, it installs as an update, so there is no need to remove the old version first, and your videos and presets stay. ClearMovie needs Android 10 or newer.

If you use [Obtainium](https://obtainium.imranr.dev/) and your ClearMovie entry still has the APK filter `arm64-v8a`, edit the entry and clear that filter. Otherwise Obtainium will not find the new file.

## Good to know

- The intro, frame and ending videos are still not part of the app. Copy your own onto the phone first, then choose them when ClearMovie asks.
- A frame you added to a video before this version may now be heard. If you do not want its sound, tap the frame, then **Volume**, or touch and hold the frame row's icon, then **Mute row**.
- In videos you made before, your videos' own sound now goes quieter under a voice you recorded, and a frame's sound under your music. To change that for a row, touch and hold its icon.
- On some phones the other voices cannot run; ClearMovie then says so and keeps your own recordings.
- If a preset has a default voice and you close the voice pack window without adding the pack, the recording stays in your own voice.
- Only one video can be saved at a time.
- If something looks wrong, please tell us on the [ClearMovie GitHub page](https://github.com/itsluminous/ClearMovie/issues).
