ClearMovie 0.0.6 fixes a crash: before this version, a video you were making that was longer than about 13 minutes made ClearMovie close as soon as you opened it, and again every time you started the app. Now it opens normally. It can also download the voice pack for you, shows what the voice engine is doing, adds Hindi HD, and keeps the screen on while a video plays or saves.

## What's new

### Fixed

- **Long videos no longer close ClearMovie.** Before this version, opening a video you were making that was longer than about 13 minutes closed ClearMovie, and it closed again on every start until its data was cleared. Now the video opens and plays as it should. Your edits were saved all along, so after the update the video is there as you left it.
- **Zooming in on a long video.** Zooming all the way in on a video longer than about 6 minutes also closed ClearMovie. It no longer does. On a video longer than about 2 minutes, **Zoom in** does not go as close as before (the longer the video, the less close), and is greyed out when it can go no closer.
- **After a crash, ClearMovie starts at Home.** If ClearMovie closes because of an error, the next time you open it, it starts at Home instead of opening the same video again. Your video stays in **My videos**.
- **Share crash details.** After such a crash, **Settings**, then **About**, shows **Share crash details**. It opens your phone's share menu, so you can send what went wrong (the app and Android versions, the phone model, the time and the technical details) to whoever you like. Nothing is sent unless you share it. Read it before you send it.

### Voices

- **The voice pack downloads in the app.** The first time a voice is needed, ClearMovie offers to download the voice pack. Tap **Download**: the window shows how much is done (and so does a notification, if ClearMovie may show notifications). Tap **Hide** to keep editing while it works. **Cancel** stops it: the file it was downloading is removed, and files it had already finished stay. If the connection drops, trying again carries on from where it stopped, and with no connection the download waits for one. Before you tap **Download**, this window tells you when you are on mobile data. Every file is checked before it is used. You can still download the files in a browser and add them, as before.
- **See what the voice engine is doing.** After you record in another voice, the voice item's panel opens at once. There, and in **Type the words**, ClearMovie says **Getting the voice engine ready...**, then **Finding the words in your recording...** or **Making the voice...**, with a progress bar. Tap **Stop** to end it. If something goes wrong, tap **Find the words** or **Make voice** again. With the panel closed, the same words show in a bar above the editor's buttons, with **Try again** when something went wrong. Until it finishes, **Record voice** in another voice, **Type the words**, and **Delete** and **Split** for that item wait.
- **Hindi HD.** Better recognition of spoken Hindi words, and two more Indian voices. On a phone with 4 GB of RAM or more, the voice pack window offers Hindi HD instead of the standard Hindi files the first time Hindi words are needed (when you record or type in Hindi), and says so. A phone with 8 GB of RAM or more gets the most accurate version (about 1.6 GB to download, about 3 GB on the phone), and a phone with 4 GB or more a lighter one (about 350 MB to download, about 0.9 GB on the phone). A phone with less RAM keeps the standard Hindi recognition, and can still use the two extra voices. If you already added Hindi in 0.0.5, get Hindi HD from **Settings**, then **Storage**. That download starts as soon as you tap **Download**, so use Wi-Fi.
- **Hindi numbers, dates and times are said as words.** When ClearMovie says Hindi words, a number or a time in them, such as 25 or 11:35, is now said in Hindi words, and a date such as 10.5.2026 as its three numbers.

### Playing and saving

- **The screen stays on.** While a video plays in ClearMovie (in the editor, when you check a preset, or the video you just saved) and while a video is being saved, the screen no longer turns off by itself, as it already did while you record your voice. This works only while ClearMovie is on the screen. When playing stops or the video is saved, your phone's usual screen timeout comes back.

## The internet, for the voice pack only

Up to 0.0.5, ClearMovie could not use the internet at all. From 0.0.6 it has internet access (Android does not ask you about this), and uses it for one thing only: downloading the voice pack and Hindi HD from ClearMovie's own files on GitHub, after you tap **Download**. ClearMovie never sends your photos, videos or recordings anywhere by itself, and uploads nothing. Like any website, GitHub sees your phone's internet address, and its model and Android version, when it sends the files. ClearMovie still has no accounts, no ads and no tracking.

## Which file to download

There is one file, `ClearMovie-0.0.6.apk`, and it works on every kind of phone. Open it on your phone to install it. If you have an older version, it installs as an update, so there is no need to remove the old version first, and your videos and presets stay. ClearMovie needs Android 10 or newer.

If you use [Obtainium](https://obtainium.imranr.dev/) and your ClearMovie entry still has the APK filter `arm64-v8a`, edit the entry and clear that filter. Otherwise Obtainium will not find the new file.

## Good to know

- If 0.0.5 kept closing on you, install 0.0.6 over it. There is no need to clear ClearMovie's data or remove the app first.
- The voice pack is about 150 MB to download. With Hindi HD it is up to about 1.7 GB. Wi-Fi is the better choice for both.
- The intro, frame and ending videos are still not part of the app. Copy your own onto the phone first, then choose them when ClearMovie asks.
- Only one video can be saved at a time.
- If something looks wrong, please tell us on the [ClearMovie GitHub page](https://github.com/itsluminous/ClearMovie/issues).
