ClearMovie turns the photos and videos from a family event into a finished video, with your intro, a frame overlay, background music, captions, transitions and a closing message. It is designed for people who have never used a video editor.

Everything an event video needs

The features are chosen for one job: turning the photos and videos from a day into a video you can share. Every button has a label, and nothing is hidden behind jargon.

Presets

An intro, a green-screen frame overlay that runs across the whole video, and an ending, set up once and reused with one tap. A new install starts with none: tap New preset on Home, choose its files and the video shape, then tap the frame's screen colour and place the picture area. Each preset keeps a caption style, including the animation captions come in with. You can replace or skip any part for a single video, and edit or delete any preset from Home.

Share from your gallery

Select photos and videos in your gallery, tap Share, then choose ClearMovie. They land on the timeline in the order they were taken.

A timeline that stays simple

Photos and videos on one row, plus as many text, music, voice, picture-in-picture and frame rows as you need. Pinch to zoom, tap “+” to add something exactly where you want it, drag either end of an item to trim it, and edit from a toolbar of big, labelled buttons. Record your voice while the video plays, and lower the music while people talk.

Captions in Hindi & English

Captions start in the preset's spot, font and size. Drag them, pull a handle on the box to resize them, recolour them, or tap Reset. Devanagari fonts are built in.

Music that steps aside

The frame's music plays under the whole video. It is turned down automatically while your videos play with their own sound, and everything is turned down for the closing message.

Transitions & animations

Crossfades, fades, wipes, slides and zooms between items, and gentle zooms or fade-ins on single photos and captions. Each one shows a live preview before you choose it.

Works in portrait and landscape

Turn the phone sideways and the editor puts the preview on the left with the timeline beside it. A wide window on a tablet, foldable or in split-screen gets the same layout. The app follows your phone's light or dark mode, and uses your wallpaper colours on Android 12+.

Autosave

Every change is saved as you go, and your work comes back if the app closes unexpectedly. Your videos wait for you under "My videos".

Fast, hardware export

Choose Best (1080p), Good (720p) or Small (480p). Saving uses your phone's video hardware and keeps going if you turn the phone or leave the app.

Private by design

No internet permission, no accounts, no analytics. Your photos and videos never leave your phone.

Three steps, start to finish

No layers, keyframes or settings screens to learn.

Pick a preset

Choose your preset, or start without one. Check the intro, frame and ending, then tap Next.

Add photos & videos

Pick them in the app, or share them straight from the gallery. Drag to reorder, tap to trim or add a caption.

Export & share

Tap the download button at the top, choose a size, and the finished video is saved to your gallery, ready to send.
